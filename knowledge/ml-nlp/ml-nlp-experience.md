# Machine Learning and NLP experience

- Trained a spaCy NER model from scratch on the CRAFT biomedical corpus (MedEntity project, research internship at FernUniversität in Hagen), covering ontology-based entity types such as chemicals, cell types, genes/gene ontology terms, proteins, taxonomy, sequences, and anatomy.
- Built a web-based annotation interface for creating training data.

## Speech processing

- Speaker diarization pipelines at Prolumios to attribute transcript segments to speakers.

## LLM-based text analysis
