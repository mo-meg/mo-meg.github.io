# Professional profile

Mohamed Megahed is an AI Engineer based in Berlin, Germany.

Professional title: AI Engineer | RAG & LLM Systems | Machine Learning | AI Engineering.

He holds a Master's degree (M.Sc. Information Systems / Business Informatics, German: Wirtschaftsinformatik) from FernUniversität in Hagen, Germany, completed in 2025.

He focuses on designing, developing, and deploying practical AI solutions. His experience covers Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), multimodal AI and multimodal retrieval systems, Large Language Models (LLMs), Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing (NLP), speech processing, and AI-powered automation.

He is interested in bridging advanced AI technologies with real-world business applications through scalable, reliable, and privacy-conscious AI architectures.
