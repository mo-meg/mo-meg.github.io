# Research internship at FernUniversität in Hagen

Type: academic research (research internship / "Fachpraktikum CIE") — not commercial employment.

Focus: cloud-based information extraction.

## Responsibilities

- Developing web-based annotation interfaces.
- Implementing spaCy-based Named Entity Recognition (NER) solutions.
- Configuring and optimizing Google Cloud Platform (GCP) resources.

## Result

The internship produced MedEntity, a spaCy NER model and application for extracting biomedical entities from scientific articles, deployed on Google Cloud Platform. Details are in the project document "Cloud-Based Information Extraction with spaCy NER (MedEntity)".
