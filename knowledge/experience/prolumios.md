## AI-powered meeting intelligence

At Prolumios, Mohamed works on AI-powered meeting intelligence solutions that are integrated into enterprise CRM systems. The goal is to turn recorded sales conversations into structured, actionable information inside the CRM where sales teams already work.

Components of the solution:

- Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) using Whisper and speech-processing pipelines.
- Speaker diarization to attribute parts of the transcript to individual speakers (who spoke when).
- NLP and LLM-based analysis of the transcript.
- Sentiment detection and theme detection.
- Sales conversation insights.
- Automated follow-up recommendations.
- AWS infrastructure.
- Python and backend integration with CRM systems.

## Pipeline

Audio → ASR (Whisper) → speaker diarization → speaker-attributed transcript → LLM analysis (sentiment, themes) → business insights and follow-up recommendations → CRM integration.
